John Deere UX

As a UX designer at John Deere, I designed UI features for embedded touchscreen systems on Combine harvesters, translating new hardware capabilities into updated interaction patterns and control layouts.

I worked with UX leads, hardware engineers, and product teams to design and ship embedded system interfaces for John Deere crop harvesting products, aligning UI decisions with embedded system constraints. I also contributed to and drove adoption of Deere’s Embedded Systems UX/UI standards, used by IT delivery teams worldwide.
