About

Software engineer with HCI depth, building scalable product interfaces.

I bring senior-level frontend judgment to product teams: turning ambiguous interaction ideas into accessible, scalable UI, partnering closely with design, and improving the systems and workflows that help teams ship with more consistency.

How I Work

My work sits at the boundary of design intent and production reality. I care about how an interaction feels, how a component scales, how edge cases behave, and how a team can move from concept to shipped product without flattening the quality of the experience.

Product Judgment

HCI training, user-centered thinking, and a strong eye for hierarchy, state, accessibility, and interaction flow.

Engineering Ownership

React, React Native, reusable UI systems, debugging, cross-platform implementation, and production-quality delivery.

Team Leverage

Clear component patterns, design-engineering alignment, mentoring, and AI-supported workflows that help teams iterate faster.
