About

Lisha Chen is a UX Engineer with an HCI background, bridging design craft and production frontend engineering.

With a background in Human-Computer Interaction and professional experience across HBO Max, eBay, and John Deere, I specialize in translating ambiguous product ideas into scalable, accessible, high-quality interfaces.

How I Work

I sit close to the boundary between design and engineering. I care about how an interaction feels, how a component scales, how an edge case behaves, and how quickly a team can move from concept to production without losing quality.

Design Sensibility

HCI training, user-centered thinking, interaction design, and a strong eye for state, hierarchy, and flow.

Engineering Execution

React, React Native, reusable UI systems, debugging, accessibility, and production implementation.

Future Interfaces

Interest in browser-based tools, rapid prototyping, and high-craft interaction patterns for emerging product concepts.
