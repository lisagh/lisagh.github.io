About

Software engineer with an HCI background, building product experiences from interaction design to production.

I work across UX, frontend systems, and product reliability: translating ambiguous interaction ideas into accessible UI, validating data and state behavior, and supporting shipped features through debugging, on-call, and cross-functional iteration.

How I Work

My work sits at the boundary of design intent and production reality. I care about how an interaction feels, whether the data and state shown to users are correct, how edge cases behave, and how shipped features hold up after launch.

Interaction Quality

HCI training, user-centered thinking, accessibility, information hierarchy, interaction flow, and polished UI states.

Production Engineering

React, React Native, reusable UI systems, data and state correctness, debugging, on-call support, and production delivery.

Cross-Functional Work

Partnering with design, product, backend, and QA to evaluate tradeoffs, unblock implementation, and iterate toward launch-ready experiences.

Experience

  • At Warner Bros. Discovery, I contribute to key HBO Max features and UI patterns across React Native and React, partnering with designers on accessible, production-quality experiences.
  • At eBay, I built reusable React interfaces and internal tools for large-scale marketing experimentation, campaign workflows, and data-heavy operational views.
  • At John Deere, I designed UX/UI solutions for embedded combine systems and improved documentation workflows for cross-functional teams.
  • My HCI graduate training gives me a research-informed foundation for usability, interaction design, prototyping, and product thinking.
